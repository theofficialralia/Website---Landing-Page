About Ralia

Ralia connects organisations, brands, products, and services with trusted promoters and influencers who spread the word through digital sharing, community networks, and real-world events.

The Role

We are seeking a highly motivated Full Stack / Mobile App Developer to build, enhance, and scale the digital infrastructure that powers the Ralia campaign matching and payout ecosystem.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop high-performance mobile and web application user interfaces.
  • Design and integrate secure APIs for user verification, wallet balances, and campaign delivery tracking.
  • Collaborate with product leads to ensure smooth, responsive end-user experiences across iOS and Android platforms.
  • Ensure web application responsiveness, reliability, and automated payout backend integrations.

Requirements

  • Proven experience building cross-platform mobile apps (Flutter, React Native, or Native iOS/Android).
  • Proficiency with full-stack web architectures, REST APIs, and modern JS frameworks.
  • Understanding of transaction security, database design, and real-time event processing.
  • Strong communication skills and independence in a fast-paced environment.
